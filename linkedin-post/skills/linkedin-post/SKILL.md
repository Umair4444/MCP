# LinkedIn Post Expert (2026)

This skill provides a comprehensive workflow for generating LinkedIn content that maximizes reach, dwell time, and authority using the latest algorithm signals.

## 1. 2026 Strategy & Algorithm Rules
- **Dwell Time:** Priority #1. Use carousels (8-10 slides) or frequent line breaks to keep users on the post.
- **Saves > Likes:** Higher weight for content that users want to reference later.
- **SEO/AEO:** Place core industry keywords in the **first 2 lines**. Structure content with clear headers and bullet points so AI models can parse and cite your expertise.
- **Hashtags:** Use exactly **3–5 hashtags** at the very bottom.
- **The "Golden Hour":** Reply to comments (15+ words) within the first 60–90 minutes.

## 2. High-Impact Hook Library
Stop the scroll in < 2 seconds using these proven formulas:
- **Contrarian:** "[Common Belief] is dead. Here’s why."
- **Curiosity Gap:** "I analyzed 500 [Items] that did [Result]. 7 patterns emerged."
- **Personal Story:** "Last Tuesday, my biggest client fired me. It was the best thing that ever happened."
- **Direct Value:** "The exact template I use to [Action]. Steal it below."

## 3. Post Templates

### A. The "Listicle" (High Dwell Time)
1. **Hook:** [Value/Curiosity Hook]
2. **Context:** Briefly explain why this matters *now*.
3. **The List:** 3–7 actionable, bulleted insights.
4. **Summary:** One-sentence takeaway.
5. **CTA:** Low-friction question (e.g., "Which of these 5 is your favorite?").

### B. The "Contrarian Pivot" (High Engagement)
1. **Hook:** [Contrarian Hook]
2. **The Problem:** Why the status quo is failing.
3. **The Insight:** Your "new way" of thinking.
4. **The Evidence:** 1–2 sentences of logic/proof.
5. **CTA:** "Do you agree or disagree? Let's discuss."

## 4. Final Optimization Checklist
- [ ] **Hook:** Forces the "See more" click.
- [ ] **Keywords:** Industry terms in the first 2 lines.
- [ ] **Formatting:** Mobile-friendly (lots of white space).
- [ ] **AEO:** Direct answer to a common professional problem.
- [ ] **Hashtags:** 3–5 tags at the very end.
- [ ] **CTA:** Specific, non-generic question.
